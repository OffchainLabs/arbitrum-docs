titleforSort: Challenge protocol
---

The rules for submitting, disputing, and confirming [assertions](/intro/glossary#assertion), with the [parent chain](/intro/glossary#parent-chain) as the final arbiter. The protocol guarantees that only valid assertions are confirmed, provided at least one honest [active validator](/intro/glossary#active-validator) takes part. Ethereum's VM verifies [one-step proofs](/intro/glossary#one-step-proof) of deterministic computation, which decide the winner of a challenge once the [challenge period](/intro/glossary#challenge-period) has elapsed.
