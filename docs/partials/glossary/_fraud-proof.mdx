---
title: Fraud proof
key: fraud-proof
titleforSort: Fraud proof
---

A proof that shows whether an invalid state transition has taken place. Challenge participants generate fraud proofs and submit them to the [parent chain](/intro/glossary#parent-chain), where a [smart contract](/intro/glossary#smart-contract) verifies them. An Arbitrum chain that settles to Ethereum therefore makes Ethereum the final arbiter of any disagreement over an [assertion](/intro/glossary#assertion). No party can falsify a fraud proof, because executing a [WASM](/intro/glossary#wasm) instruction on a given pre-state has exactly one correct result. Challenges execute a variant of WASM called [WAVM](/intro/glossary#wavm).
