A proprietary automated market maker. A constant-product automated market maker relies on arbitrageurs to correct its prices. A propAMM instead updates its onchain quotes from an external trusted price source, and usually supplies its own liquidity. propAMMs need cheap, frequent, priority-ordered inclusion to keep those quotes current, so they depend on an ordering policy that sells priority, such as PGA.
