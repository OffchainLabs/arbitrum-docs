---
title: Priority Fee
key: priority-fee
titleforSort: Priority Fee
---

The per-gas tip you add on top of the base fee to bid for earlier inclusion, set in the `max_priority_fee_per_gas` field of an [EIP-1559](/intro/glossary#eip-1559) transaction. The chain computes the effective value as `min(max_priority_fee_per_gas, max_fee_per_gas - base_fee_per_gas)`. Under PGA, the [Sequencer](/intro/glossary#sequencer) orders transactions by this value, highest first.
