---
title: Permissionless validation
key: permissionless-validation
titleforSort: Permissionless validation
---

The ability for anyone to post [assertions](/intro/glossary#assertion) to the [Rollup contract](/intro/glossary#rollup-contract) and challenge the assertions of others, without needing permission. Before [BoLD](/intro/glossary#bold), the Rollup contract held an allowlist of validators. BoLD removes that allowlist, because it bounds how long a [delay attack](/intro/glossary#delay-attack) can postpone confirmation.
