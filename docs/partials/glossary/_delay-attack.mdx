---
title: Delay attack
key: delay-attack
titleforSort: Delay attack
---

An attack in which one or more malicious parties follow the [challenge protocol](/intro/glossary#challenge-protocol) to delay [confirmation](/intro/glossary#confirmation) of results to the [parent chain](/intro/glossary#parent-chain). The protocol used before [BoLD](/intro/glossary#bold) allowed this, because an honest party had to play a separate 1-vs-1 challenge against each adversary. BoLD sets a constant upper bound on confirmation time: one honest validator defends a single assertion against any number of malicious claims. Solving delay attacks is what makes [permissionless validation](/intro/glossary#permissionless-validation) safe.
