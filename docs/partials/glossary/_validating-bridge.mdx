---
title: Validating bridge
key: validating-bridge
titleforSort: Validating bridge
---

The [bridge](/intro/glossary#bridge) smart contract that uses the [parent chain](/intro/glossary#parent-chain)'s security and censorship resistance to unlock bridged assets. Assets unlock only after an [assertion](/intro/glossary#assertion) showing that the assets left the [child chain](/intro/glossary#child-chain) is posted and confirmed.
