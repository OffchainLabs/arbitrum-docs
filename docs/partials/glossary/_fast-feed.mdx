titleforSort: Fast Feed
---

A paid, authenticated data stream on [Arbitrum One](/intro/glossary#arbitrum-one) that publishes individual transactions, their relative ordering, and related metadata after the [Sequencer](/intro/glossary#sequencer) orders them, and before the block that contains them is published on the [Sequencer Feed](/intro/glossary#sequencer-feed). Subscribers buy timed access through the Fast Feed Payment Contract.
