id: fast-feed
title: 'Fast Feed'
---

A paid, authenticated data stream on [Arbitrum One](/glossary#arbitrum-one) that publishes individual transactions, their relative ordering, and related metadata after the [Sequencer](/glossary#sequencer) orders and executes them, and before the block that contains them is published on the [Sequencer Feed](/glossary#sequencer-feed). Subscribers buy timed access through the `Tickets` contract, also called the Fast Feed Payment Contract.
