id: state-pruning
title: 'State Pruning'
---

Removing historical [chain state](/glossary#chain-state) a node no longer needs, to keep its database from growing without bound. On [HashDB](/glossary#hashdb) you prune manually with `--init.prune` and the node is offline while it runs. On [PathDB](/glossary#pathdb) Nitro prunes online as the node runs, with no intervention and no downtime. An [archive node](/glossary#archive-node) prunes nothing by definition.
