---
id: validator
title: 'Validator'
---

An [Arbitrum Full Node](/glossary#arbitrum-full-node) that tracks the status of the chains' [Assertion](/glossary#assertion)s. A validator may be a [Watchtower Validator](/glossary#watchtower-validator), a [Defensive Validator](/glossary#defensive-validator), or an [Active Validator](/glossary#active-validator).
