id: challenge-period
title: 'Challenge Period'
---

The window during which anyone can challenge an [assertion](/docs/glossary#assertion), after which the assertion can be confirmed. On Arbitrum One it is [6.4 days](/docs/arbitrum-essentials/reference/chain-params). The [Arbitrum DAO](/docs/glossary#arbitrum-dao) can configure this value. A [BoLD](/docs/glossary#bold) challenge adds at most one more challenge period before an assertion confirms.
