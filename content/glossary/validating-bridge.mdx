title: 'Validating bridge'
---

The [bridge](/docs/glossary#bridge) smart contract that uses the [parent chain](/docs/glossary#parent-chain)'s security and censorship resistance to unlock bridged assets. Assets unlock only after the chain posts and confirms an [assertion](/docs/glossary#assertion) showing that the assets left the [child chain](/docs/glossary#child-chain).
