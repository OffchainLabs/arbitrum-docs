id: fraud-proof
title: 'Fraud proof'
---

A proof that an invalid state transition took place. Challenge participants generate fraud proofs and submit them to the [parent chain](/docs/glossary#parent-chain), where a [smart contract](/docs/glossary#smart-contract) verifies them. An Arbitrum chain that settles to Ethereum therefore makes Ethereum the final arbiter of any disagreement over an [assertion](/docs/glossary#assertion). No party can falsify a fraud proof, because executing a [WASM](/docs/glossary#wasm) instruction on a given pre-state has exactly one correct result. Challenges execute a variant of WASM called [WAVM](/docs/glossary#wavm).
