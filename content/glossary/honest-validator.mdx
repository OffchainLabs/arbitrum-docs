title: 'Honest validator'
---

A [validator](/glossary#validator) that knows the correct state of the [child chain](/glossary#child-chain) and acts on it. It creates [assertions](/glossary#assertion), confirms valid ones, and challenges invalid ones. It runs an [Arbitrum full node](/glossary#arbitrum-full-node) in `MakeNodes`, `Defensive`, `StakeLatest`, or `ResolveNodes` mode. Every chain needs at least one [proposer](/glossary#proposer) in `MakeNodes` mode to advance the chain.
