title: 'Stylus'
---

Upgrade to the [Arbitrum Nitro](/glossary#arbitrum-nitro) virtual machine that lets you write smart contracts in languages like Rust, C, and C++ by taking advantage of Nitro's use of WASM. Stylus shipped in [ArbOS 32](/run-a-node/arbos-releases/arbos32) and runs on Arbitrum One, Arbitrum Nova, and Arbitrum chains ([read more](/stylus/gentle-introduction)).
