---
id: proposer
title: 'Proposer'
---

A [Validator](/glossary#validator) configured to actively submit new [Assertions](/glossary#assertion) to the parent chain under [BoLD](/glossary#bold). Proposers post the largest bonds in the staking hierarchy to deter delay attacks. Contrast with a [Watchtower Validator](/glossary#watchtower-validator), which only observes, and a [Defensive Validator](/glossary#defensive-validator), which acts only to counter an invalid claim.
