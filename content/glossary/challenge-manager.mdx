id: challenge-manager
title: 'ChallengeManager'
---

The [BoLD](/glossary#bold) smart contract on the [parent chain](/glossary#parent-chain) that starts challenges on [assertions](/glossary#assertion) and lets anyone join a challenge without permission. It holds the entry points for making challenge moves, opening [edges](/glossary#edge), creating sub-challenges, and confirming challenges. It is written in Solidity and is new in BoLD.
