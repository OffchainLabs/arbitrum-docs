title: 'Validator client'
---

The software an [honest validator](/docs/glossary#honest-validator) runs. It learns the correct [child chain](/docs/glossary#child-chain) history from a [state manager backend](/docs/glossary#state-manager-backend), then posts [assertions](/docs/glossary#assertion) to the [parent chain](/docs/glossary#parent-chain) by bonding a claim. It reaches the contracts through [chain bindings](/docs/glossary#chain-bindings). It tracks the [Rollup contract](/docs/glossary#rollup-contract) for posted assertions and, if you configure it to do so, opens challenges against invalid ones. Under [BoLD](/docs/glossary#bold) it also joins challenges that other honest validators started. Its goal is that only honest assertions are confirmed, and that every honest assertion is confirmed within at most two [challenge periods](/docs/glossary#challenge-period). Every [Arbitrum full node](/docs/glossary#arbitrum-full-node) is a [watchtower validator](/docs/glossary#watchtower-validator) by default: it posts nothing, but warns you when it detects an invalid assertion.
_Also known as: validator software_
