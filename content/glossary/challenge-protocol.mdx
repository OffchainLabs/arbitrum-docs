id: challenge-protocol
title: 'Challenge protocol'
---

The rules for submitting, disputing, and confirming [assertions](/docs/glossary#assertion), with the [parent chain](/docs/glossary#parent-chain) as the final arbiter. The protocol guarantees that only valid assertions are confirmed, provided at least one honest [active validator](/docs/glossary#active-validator) takes part. Ethereum's VM verifies [one-step proofs](/docs/glossary#one-step-proof) of deterministic computation, which decide the winner of a challenge once the [challenge period](/docs/glossary#challenge-period) has elapsed.
