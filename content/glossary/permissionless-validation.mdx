title: 'Permissionless validation'
---

The ability for anyone to post [assertions](/glossary#assertion) to the [Rollup contract](/glossary#rollup-contract) and challenge the assertions of others. Before [BoLD](/glossary#bold), the Rollup contract held an allowlist of validators. BoLD removes that allowlist, because it bounds how long a [delay attack](/glossary#delay-attack) can postpone confirmation.
