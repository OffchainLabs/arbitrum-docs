id: arbitrum-expansion-program
title: 'Arbitrum Expansion Program (AEP)'
---

The licensing framework under which third parties can deploy new [Arbitrum chains](/glossary#arbitrum-chain) outside [Arbitrum One](/glossary#arbitrum-one) and [Arbitrum Nova](/glossary#arbitrum-nova). In exchange for permissionless deployment, the chain remits 10% of net revenue—8% to the [Arbitrum DAO](/glossary#arbitrum-dao) and 2% to ecosystem programs.
