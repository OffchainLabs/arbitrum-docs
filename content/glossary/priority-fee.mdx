id: priority-fee
title: 'Priority Fee'
---

The per-gas tip you add on top of the base fee to bid for earlier inclusion, set in the `max_priority_fee_per_gas` field of an [EIP-1559](/docs/glossary#eip-1559) transaction. The chain computes the effective value as `min(max_priority_fee_per_gas, max_fee_per_gas - base_fee_per_gas)`. Under PGA, the [Sequencer](/docs/glossary#sequencer) orders transactions by this value, highest first.
