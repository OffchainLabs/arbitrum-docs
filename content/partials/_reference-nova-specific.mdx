### FAQ

#### What changed on January 31, 2026?

These third-party tools no longer support Arbitrum Nova:

- Alchemy
- Nova Arbiscan ([nova.arbiscan.io](https://nova.arbiscan.io))
- Tenderly

The change applies only to Arbitrum Nova. It does not affect Arbitrum One or other Arbitrum chains.

#### Does this change affect my funds or assets on Arbitrum Nova?

No. The change does not affect your funds or onchain assets on Arbitrum Nova. You can still access and withdraw all of your assets.

#### Do existing Nova apps still work?

Yes, if you move off the deprecated tools and update your infrastructure dependencies. Review where your decentralized app uses these services, and move to alternative providers. The deadline for this move was January 31, 2026.
