## Arbitrum public RPC endpoints

Use these public RPC endpoints to connect to Arbitrum One, Arbitrum Nova, and Arbitrum Sepolia.

<Callout type="warn">

- Unlike the RPC URLs, the <Term id="sequencer">sequencer</Term> endpoints support only `eth_sendRawTransaction` and [`eth_sendRawTransactionConditional`](/json-rpc-spike/eth_sendRawTransactionConditional) calls.
- The Arbitrum public RPC endpoints do not support WebSocket.
- The Arbitrum public RPC endpoints do not support IPv6.

</Callout>

| Name                           | RPC URL                                  | Chain ID | Block explorer                                                                                   | Underlying chain | Tech stack       | Sequencer feed URL                      | Sequencer endpoint                                 |
| ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------- | ---------------- | --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| **Arbitrum One**               | `https://arb1.arbitrum.io/rpc`           | 42161    | [Arbiscan](https://arbiscan.io/), [Blockscout](https://arbitrum.blockscout.com/)                 | Ethereum         | Nitro (Rollup)   | `wss://arb1-feed.arbitrum.io/feed`      | `https://arb1-sequencer.arbitrum.io/rpc`           |
| **Arbitrum Nova**              | `https://nova.arbitrum.io/rpc`           | 42170    | [Blockscout](https://arbitrum-nova.blockscout.com/)                                              | Ethereum         | Nitro (AnyTrust) | `wss://nova-feed.arbitrum.io/feed`      | `https://nova-sequencer.arbitrum.io/rpc`           |
| **Arbitrum Sepolia (Testnet)** | `https://sepolia-rollup.arbitrum.io/rpc` | 421614   | [Arbiscan](https://sepolia.arbiscan.io/), [Blockscout](https://arbitrum-sepolia.blockscout.com/) | Sepolia          | Nitro (Rollup)   | `wss://sepolia-rollup.arbitrum.io/feed` | `https://sepolia-rollup-sequencer.arbitrum.io/rpc` |
