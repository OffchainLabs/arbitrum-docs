<Callout type="warn" title="Protect your wallet from scams">

- Keep your secret recovery phrase (seed phrase) and your private key to yourself. If someone asks for them, that person wants to steal your funds.
- Use the bridge at [bridge.arbitrum.io](https://bridge.arbitrum.io/). Check the address in your browser before you connect your wallet.
- Messages that offer rewards, airdrops, yield programs, or invitation codes in the name of Arbitrum can be scams. Connect your wallet only to sites whose address you typed or bookmarked yourself.
- Scammers send direct messages that pretend to be Arbitrum support. Ask for help in the public channels of the [Arbitrum Discord](https://discord.gg/arbitrum).

</Callout>
