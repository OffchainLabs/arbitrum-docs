The [Arbitrum Security Council](https://docs.arbitrum.foundation/concepts/security-council) has temporarily paused new <Term id="stylus">Stylus</Term> contract <Term id="activation">activations</Term> on <Term id="arbitrum-one">Arbitrum One</Term> and <Term id="arbitrum-nova">Arbitrum Nova</Term> as a security measure. To date, all Stylus findings from contracts that have been reviewed have only posed a denial-of-service risk to chain liveness and no user funds have been at risk.

<Callout type="warn" title="What this means for you">

- You cannot activate new or expired Stylus contracts on Arbitrum One or Arbitrum Nova while the pause is in effect.
- Already activated Stylus contracts keep running until they [expire](/stylus/concepts/activation#programtimeleft). Calling them remains permissionless.
- You can renew an active Stylus contract before it expires with the [keepalive mechanism](/stylus/concepts/activation#expiration-and-keepalive).
- Solidity and EVM contract deployment and execution are unaffected.

</Callout>

To learn why the Security Council took this action, read the forum post [Security Council Emergency Action – 2/10/2026](https://forum.arbitrum.foundation/t/security-council-emergency-action-2-10-2026/31530).

## What is paused

The pause applies to the activation step only. Activation is the step that makes a Stylus contract callable. To learn how deployment and activation work, refer to [Activation](/stylus/concepts/activation).

- You cannot activate a new Stylus contract. You also cannot reactivate a Stylus contract that has expired.
- Stylus contracts that are active keep running until they expire. An activation lasts 365 days by default. To read the time left, call [`programTimeLeft`](/stylus/concepts/activation#programtimeleft).
- You can renew an active Stylus contract before it expires. Call [`codehashKeepalive`](/stylus/concepts/activation#codehashkeepalive). By default, the contract must be at least 31 days old.

## What comes next

We remain confident in Stylus, which lets chain owners customize their networks and developers optimize key contracts. The Arbitrum Foundation will work with the <Term id="arbitrum-dao">ArbitrumDAO</Term> on next steps for reopening activations in a way that preserves these uses while restricting hand-crafted WASM programs.
