<Term id="arbos">ArbOS</Term> 61 "Elara" is active on Arbitrum Sepolia, <Term id="arbitrum-one">Arbitrum One</Term>, and <Term id="arbitrum-nova">Arbitrum Nova</Term>. It activated on Arbitrum One and Arbitrum Nova on Thursday, August 20, 2026.

<Callout type="warn" title="Action required">

If you run a node on these chains, you **must run** Nitro **`v3.11.3`** or higher to sync.

- **Docker image:** [offchainlabs/nitro-node:v3.11.3-beb2108](https://hub.docker.com/r/offchainlabs/nitro-node/tags?name=v3.11.3-beb2108)

- **<Term id="wasm-module-root">WASM module root</Term>:** `0xc10cd7ec6acaf1c441a3f6bd0900ad20f15855ba775a96f1939118cbc629dc97` ([consensus-v61](https://github.com/OffchainLabs/nitro/releases/tag/consensus-v61))

</Callout>

## Actions required for node operators

### Sepolia network

ArbOS 61 activated on the Arbitrum Sepolia chain on Monday, June 29, 2026, at 15:00 UTC.

### Arbitrum One and Arbitrum Nova

The ArbitrumDAO approved [ArbOS 61](/run-a-node/arbos-releases/arbos61) for Arbitrum One and Arbitrum Nova in a Constitutional onchain vote. After the waiting periods and phases set out in the ArbitrumDAO Constitution, ArbOS 61 activated on both chains on Thursday, August 20, 2026.

The [Arbitrum DAO network upgrades table](https://docs.arbitrum.foundation/network-upgrades) records the exact activation time of each chain.

## Actions for Arbitrum chain owners

ArbOS 61 has run on Arbitrum One for more than 30 days. You can upgrade your <Term id="arbitrum-chain">Arbitrum chain</Term> now.

To upgrade, follow [How to upgrade ArbOS on your Arbitrum chain](/launch-arbitrum-chain/operate/arbos-upgrade) and the [nitro-contracts upgrade instructions](https://github.com/OffchainLabs/arbitrum-chain-actions?tab=readme-ov-file#nitro-contracts-upgrades). Use the Nitro version, Docker image, and WASM module root listed above.

ArbOS 61 lets your chain collect priority fees. Do not enable <Term id="priority-gas-auction">Priority Gas Auctions (PGA)</Term> or priority fee collection on your chain yet. Offchain Labs updates this notice when chain operators can enable them.

<Callout type="error" title="Do not enable multi-dimensional gas pricing">

Do not call `setMultiGasPricingConstraints` on the `ArbOwner` precompile. ArbOS 61 ships multi-dimensional gas pricing in the node software, but a future Nitro version removes it. If your chain has it enabled when that version ships, your chain forks.

You can now use multi-constraint pricing with a single gas dimension. To configure it, call `setGasPricingConstraints` as described in [Dynamic Pricing for Arbitrum chains](/launch-arbitrum-chain/chain-config/costs/dynamic-pricing).

</Callout>

### Update the L1 pricing parameters for Ethereum Glamsterdam

Ethereum's Glamsterdam upgrade changes the gas cost of posting batches to Ethereum. If your chain posts its batches to Ethereum, you must update two L1 pricing parameters after Glamsterdam activates on Ethereum mainnet. Without the update, the L1 fee your users pay no longer matches what your <Term id="batch-poster">batch poster</Term> spends.

Offchain Labs proposed the same update for Arbitrum One and Arbitrum Nova in the [Glamsterdam L1 pricing AIP](https://forum.arbitrum.foundation/t/constitutional-aip-update-arbitrum-one-and-nova-l1-pricing-parameters-for-ethereum-glamsterdam/31555). Trail of Bits audited the governance payload. The [audit summary](/audit-reports/2026_10_19_trail_of_bits_glamsterdam_gas_parameters_summary_report.pdf) is on the [audit reports](/audit-reports) page.

| Parameter                | Current value | New value |
| ------------------------ | ------------- | --------- |
| `parentGasFloorPerToken` | `10`          | `16`      |
| `perBatchGasCharge`      | `210,000`     | `480,000` |

After Glamsterdam is live on Ethereum, call the `ArbOwner` precompile from a <Term id="chain-owner">chain owner</Term> account:

```shell
cast send --rpc-url $CHILD_CHAIN_RPC --private-key $OWNER_KEY 0x0000000000000000000000000000000000000070 "setParentGasFloorPerToken(uint64)" 16
cast send --rpc-url $CHILD_CHAIN_RPC --private-key $OWNER_KEY 0x0000000000000000000000000000000000000070 "setPerBatchGasCharge(int64)" 480000
```

Do not apply the new values before Glamsterdam activates. Until then, the current values match what your batch poster pays. The recommended values come from measured Arbitrum One and Nova batch costs after Glamsterdam. If your chain posts calldata instead of blobs, or posts batches of a very different size, measure your own posting costs before you pick a value. To learn how the two parameters work, refer to [Parent chain data fee pricing](/launch-arbitrum-chain/chain-config/costs/parent-chain-data-fee-pricing) and [Configure and optimize gas](/launch-arbitrum-chain/chain-config/costs/gas-optimization).

Ethereum has not announced the mainnet activation date for Glamsterdam. Offchain Labs updates this notice when the date is official.

## Context

ArbOS 61 "Elara" builds upon [ArbOS 51 "Dia"](/run-a-node/arbos-releases/arbos51). It makes two changes to Arbitrum One and Nova: it raises the <Term id="stylus">Stylus</Term> contract code size limit to 96 KB, and it adds a `BaseFeeManager` contract that manages the minimum L2 base fee.

ArbOS 61 also ships three optional features. A <Term id="chain-owner">chain owner</Term> must enable each one explicitly:

- **Alternative Data Availability (AltDA) Layer API.** Disabled on Arbitrum One and Nova. It requires [nitro-contracts v3.2.0](https://github.com/OffchainLabs/nitro-contracts/releases/tag/v3.2.0) or higher. To learn how to build a custom DA provider against the new API, refer to [How to integrate with the DA API](/launch-arbitrum-chain/extend-the-protocol/da-api-guide).
- **Compliance transaction filtering.** Disabled on Arbitrum One and Nova. On a live chain, filtering takes effect seven days after the chain owner enables it. To learn how transaction filtering works, refer to [Compliance filtering](/launch-arbitrum-chain/chain-config/sequencer/compliance-filtering).
- **Priority fee collection.** Enabled on Arbitrum One since September 23, 2026, together with PGA. Disabled on Nova. To learn how to enable it, refer to [Priority fee collection](/launch-arbitrum-chain/chain-config/costs/priority-fees).

ArbOS 61 corrects two interacting bugs in the gas refund logic that were found while testing ArbOS 60 on Arbitrum Sepolia. The fixes change the State Transition Function (STF), so they required a new ArbOS version. ArbOS 60 never activated on Arbitrum One or Nova, so neither chain was affected.

ArbOS 61 passed a Snapshot temperature check and then a Constitutional onchain vote, which ran from July 16 to July 30, 2026. To learn more about the proposal, refer to the [ArbOS 61 Elara AIP](https://forum.arbitrum.foundation/t/constitutional-aip-arbos-61-elara/30601).
