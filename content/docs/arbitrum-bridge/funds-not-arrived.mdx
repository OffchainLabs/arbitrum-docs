## A deposit has not arrived

A deposit usually arrives on the destination chain within 15 to 30 minutes, depending on chain congestion. If it takes longer, do these steps:

1. Set your wallet to the destination network. Your wallet shows the balance of one network at a time. For the network details and chain IDs, see [Add the preferred network to your wallet](/arbitrum-bridge/quickstart#step-2-add-the-preferred-network-to-your-wallet).
2. If you deposited an ERC-20 token, import the token in your wallet. A wallet shows a token balance only after you add the token.
3. Open the [Arbitrum bridge](https://bridge.arbitrum.io/), click your profile in the top-right corner, and open the **Transactions** tab. Check the status of your deposit.
4. If the deposit failed on the destination chain, check it with the [Retryables tool](https://portal.arbitrum.io/build/retryables). A token deposit is a <Term id="retryable-ticket">retryable ticket</Term>, and you can redeem a failed one there.

## A withdrawal has not arrived

A withdrawal from <Term id="arbitrum-one">Arbitrum One</Term> or Arbitrum Nova to Ethereum takes at least seven days. This wait is the <Term id="challenge-period">challenge period</Term>. The bridge shows a countdown of 7 to 8 days.

When the countdown ends, you must claim your funds on Ethereum:

1. Open the [Arbitrum bridge](https://bridge.arbitrum.io/), click your profile in the top-right corner, and open the **Transactions** tab.
2. Find your withdrawal. If the countdown is complete, press **Claim**.
3. Confirm the claim in your wallet. The claim is a transaction on Ethereum, so you need ETH on Ethereum to pay for it.

If your withdrawal is not in the **Transactions** tab, search for it by its transaction hash in the [bridge transaction history](https://portal.arbitrum.io/bridge?tab=tx_history). This happens when you withdrew from a smart contract wallet, or from an address that you do not control on Ethereum.

You cannot cancel a withdrawal after you start it. For details on the wait, see [How long does it take before I receive my funds](/arbitrum-bridge/troubleshooting#how-long-does-it-take-before-i-receive-my-funds-when-i-initiate-a-withdrawal-from-arbitrum-chains-one-and-nova).

## You sent funds to an exchange

If you sent funds to a deposit address at an exchange, the exchange controls that address. Contact the support team of the exchange and give them the transaction hash. Tell them which network you sent the funds on.

## Get help

If the steps on this page did not solve your problem, ask in the [Arbitrum Discord](https://discord.gg/arbitrum). Give this information in your message:

- The transaction hash
- The source network and the destination network
- The token and the amount

Keep your secret recovery phrase and your private key to yourself. Nobody needs them to help you.
