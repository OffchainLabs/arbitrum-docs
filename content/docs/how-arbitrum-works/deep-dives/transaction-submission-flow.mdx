This diagram is for app developers who want to know what happens after they submit a <Term id="transaction">transaction</Term> on Arbitrum One, and when it is final. The first tab follows a transaction that you send to the <Term id="sequencer">Sequencer</Term>. The second tab follows a <Term id="retryable-ticket">retryable ticket</Term> that you send from the <Term id="parent-chain">parent chain</Term>. Press **Next** and **Back**, or the arrow keys while the diagram has focus, to light the processes and contracts of each step. Select **Show details** on the card below the diagram to see the code and what you see when the step fails.
