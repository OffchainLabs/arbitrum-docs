Your <Term id="arbitrum-chain">Arbitrum chain</Term> accepts deposits of its gas token, ETH or a custom gas token, from the moment you deploy it: users deposit through the chain's Inbox contract. For ERC-20 tokens and for chains other than your parent chain, you set up a bridge. The Arbitrum stack has no token bridging standard at the protocol level. Instead, you choose one or more of the options on this page, and you can combine them.

Each option has a different owner. You deploy some options yourself. A token issuer, Circle, or a bridge provider deploys the others. The tables on this page show who acts for each option.

## Which option fits

Start from what you want your chain to do:

- **Move ERC-20 tokens between the parent chain and your chain.** Deploy the [canonical token bridge](/launch-arbitrum-chain/deploy/token-bridge). Most chains start here.
- **Earn yield on the tokens that the canonical bridge holds.** Evaluate a [Yield Bridge](/launch-arbitrum-chain/deploy/yield-bridge), a variant of the canonical token bridge.
- **Offer USDC.** Deploy [bridged USDC](/launch-arbitrum-chain/integrations/bridged-usdc), so that Circle can later upgrade it to native USDC.
- **Use an interop token as your gas token.** Configure a [native mint/burn gas token](/launch-arbitrum-chain/chain-config/costs/configure-native-mint-burn), such as a LayerZero OFT, an xERC20 token, or native USDC.
- **Reach chains other than your parent chain.** Work with a [third-party bridge](#third-party-bridges).
- **Let users bridge from a web app.** [Add your chain to the Arbitrum bridge](/launch-arbitrum-chain/integrations/bridge-ui).

## Lock-and-mint and burn-and-mint

The options use one of two models to move a token.

![Two ways a token reaches your Arbitrum chain. Left, lock-and-mint with the canonical token bridge: a gateway on the parent chain locks the token, and a gateway on your Arbitrum chain mints a copy. Deposits and withdrawals move the token only between the parent chain and your chain. Right, burn-and-mint with LayerZero OFT, xERC20 or native USDC: the token issuer's bridge connects the parent chain, your Arbitrum chain and other chains. The bridge burns the token on the source chain and mints it on the destination chain.](/img/arb-chain-bridge-token-models.svg)

- **Lock-and-mint.** The canonical token bridge locks the token in a <Term id="token-gateway">token gateway</Term> on the <Term id="parent-chain">parent chain</Term>, and mints a copy on your chain. The copy moves only between your chain and the parent chain. The bridge uses your chain's own parent-to-child and child-to-parent messages, so it adds no third-party trust.
- **Burn-and-mint.** The token issuer allows one or more bridges to burn the token on one chain and mint it on another. The token is the same on every chain, so it can reach any chain that the issuer's bridge connects. Your chain then also trusts that bridge.

## Compare the options

| Option                                                                                             | What moves                                                               | Model                                                  | Who deploys it                                                                                                          | Requirements                                                                                                             |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| [Canonical token bridge](/launch-arbitrum-chain/deploy/token-bridge)                               | ERC-20 tokens, including WETH, between the parent chain and your chain   | Lock-and-mint, over your chain's own messages          | You, with the Chain SDK, after your chain and a node run                                                                | A running chain                                                                                                          |
| [Yield Bridge](/launch-arbitrum-chain/deploy/yield-bridge)                                         | ERC-20 tokens; withdrawals return vault shares                           | Lock-and-mint; the locked tokens earn yield in a vault | You, with hands-on support that you request in the Arbitrum Discord                                                     | New token bridges only; you or a curator manage the vault                                                                |
| [Bridged USDC](/launch-arbitrum-chain/integrations/bridged-usdc)                                   | USDC                                                                     | Lock-and-mint, upgradeable to native USDC              | You deploy bridged USDC; Circle and you must both agree to move to native USDC                                          | New chains only, not chains that the Arbitrum DAO governs; Circle's bridged USDC standard                                |
| [Native mint/burn gas token](/launch-arbitrum-chain/chain-config/costs/configure-native-mint-burn) | Your chain's gas token: a LayerZero OFT, an xERC20 token, or native USDC | Burn-and-mint through the token issuer's bridge        | You deploy the bridge adapter, for example a LayerZero OFT; the <Term id="chain-owner">chain owner</Term> allowlists it | ArbOS 41 or later, ArbOS 51 recommended; `nitro-contracts` v3.1.0 and Nitro v3.9.3 or later; you enable it after genesis |
| [Third-party bridges](#third-party-bridges)                                                        | Tokens and messages, to chains beyond the parent chain                   | Depends on the provider                                | The bridge provider, by its own process                                                                                 | Set by each provider                                                                                                     |

<Callout type="warn" title="A third-party bridge adds trust">

If a third-party bridge mints your chain's gas token, your chain's liveness depends on that bridge. By default, your chain issues its gas token only for deposits through its own Inbox and Bridge contracts. See [Use native interop token with mint/burn as your Arbitrum chain gas token](/launch-arbitrum-chain/chain-config/costs/configure-native-mint-burn) for the security checklist.

</Callout>

## Third-party bridges

A third-party bridge does not work on your chain by default. Each provider decides whether and how it supports a new chain. Offchain Labs does not endorse or rank these providers. Contact each provider to learn its current process.

| Provider                                           | How it works                                                                                                                                  | How your chain gets support                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| -------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [LayerZero](/third-party-docs/LayerZero/layerzero) | Messaging protocol. Omnichain fungible tokens (OFT) burn on one chain and mint on another.                                                    | Contact LayerZero to ask for support for your chain. See the [LayerZero documentation](https://docs.layerzero.network/).                                                                                                              |
| Hyperlane                                          | Messaging protocol. Warp Routes move tokens between chains.                                                                                   | Anyone can deploy the Hyperlane core contracts to an EVM chain with the Hyperlane CLI. See [Deploy to a new chain](https://docs.hyperlane.xyz/docs/guides/chains/deploy-hyperlane).                                                   |
| Wormhole                                           | Messaging protocol verified by a network of Guardians. Native Token Transfers (NTT) move a token in burn-and-mint or hub-and-spoke mode.      | Wormhole Guardians must observe your chain, so Wormhole must add your chain to the chains it connects. See [Native Token Transfers](https://wormhole.com/docs/products/token-transfers/native-token-transfers/guides/deploy-to-evm/). |
| Across                                             | Intent-based bridge. Relayers fill a transfer on the destination chain, and Across repays them later.                                         | Across sets its own requirements for a new chain. Contact Across. See the [Across documentation](https://docs.across.to/).                                                                                                            |
| Everclear (formerly Connext)                       | Settles intents across chains. It nets intents that move in opposite directions, settles xERC20 transfers, and sends messages with Hyperlane. | See the [Everclear documentation](https://docs.everclear.org/).                                                                                                                                                                       |

xERC20 ([ERC-7281](https://github.com/connext/ERCs/blob/ERC-7281/ERCS/erc-7281.md)) is a draft token standard, not a bridge. The token issuer allows each bridge to mint and burn the token, and sets a rate limit for each bridge.

For more projects that support Arbitrum chains, see the [Arbitrum ecosystem portal](https://portal.arbitrum.io/chains/ecosystem).
